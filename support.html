LocalReader Support

Help with importing, reading, and listening.

Supported files

  • Chinese or English TXT in UTF-8, UTF-16, GBK, GB18030, or GB2312
  • Unencrypted, DRM-free EPUB with Chinese, English, or mixed-language text

Common tasks

  • Import one or more books from Tonight or Library, or open a supported file from Files.
  • Use Read and Listen inside a book to continue from the same position.
  • Start a timed story, bilingual relaxation guide, or offline sleep sound.
  • Adjust narration speed, repeat, ambience, volume, and the sleep timer.
  • Swipe an imported book to delete it.

Contact

Email pumclong@gmail.com. Include the iPhone model, iOS version, app version, file type, and steps that reproduce the problem. Do not attach a copyrighted book unless you have permission to share it.
