Privacy Policy

Effective September 28, 2026

Data collection

LocalReader does not show advertising or track users across apps. Apple and RevenueCat process purchase history and an anonymous app identifier to validate purchases, restore access, and provide purchase reporting. Book text, reading history, and sleep-session content are not sent to RevenueCat.

Books and reading history

Imported books, reading and listening progress, sleep-session history, voice settings, and app settings remain in LocalReader's storage on the iPhone. LocalReader does not upload this information to a server.

On-device voice

Kokoro ANE and Apple Voice process narration on the iPhone. LocalReader does not provide cloud text-to-speech. When a Kokoro language model is not installed, LocalReader downloads model files from the model host; the request contains no imported book text, reading history, or narration content.

File access

LocalReader accesses a TXT or EPUB file only after the user selects it in Apple's document picker or opens it with LocalReader from Files.

Retention and deletion

Imported books remain on the iPhone until the user deletes them. Deleting LocalReader removes local books, progress, and settings, but does not delete Apple or RevenueCat purchase records. Contact us for purchase-data privacy requests.
